🎯 What it does (and doesn't do)

✓ DOES

  • Owns shares of Investment Co, PMC, AMC
  • Sits as group parent for governance
  • Lets you sell/restructure cleanly later
  • Provides liability ring-fencing

✗ DOES NOT

  • Take investor money (Investment Co does)
  • Build anything (PMC does)
  • Operate the facility (AMC does)
  • Sign vendor contracts
  • Have operational employees

Mental model: The Holding Co is a clean "folder" that contains the other folders. It does nothing operational — it just owns.

💡 Why not just use EXCEED REAL ESTATE LLC?

EXCEED REAL ESTATE has its own existing business and partners. Two problems if we reuse it as Holding Co:

⚠ Risk mixing

If EXCEED REAL ESTATE has any legal/financial issue, AYUVA investors get dragged into it. Their investment is tied to all of EXCEED's other dealings.

⚠ DD complexity

Future investors would have to investigate ALL of EXCEED's history (multi-year, multi-deal). With a fresh "EXCEED Capital" they only investigate AYUVA.

→ Solution: create EXCEED Capital as a fresh, dedicated entity.

📋 Key Requirements (what we need to decide / provide)

CVML lists 4 baseline requirements. Each has a concrete decision or output we need to give them before they can incorporate the entity.

1
Minimum share capital
📌 RAK ICC research finding
RAK ICC has NO statutory minimum share capital requirement. You just declare an "authorized capital" amount on paper — it doesn't need to be paid up or deposited. Common practice:
  • Bare minimum: AED 1,000 (just a number)
  • Typical: AED 10,000–50,000
  • Better banking optics: USD 10,000–100,000 declared (~AED 37k–370k)
AED ≈ USD 13,615
Suggested default: AED 50,000 — reasonable, no paid-up requirement. You can change this anytime.
2
Shareholder structure
📌 RAK ICC rules
  • Individuals OR companies can be shareholders
  • Minimum 1 shareholder, maximum ~50
  • The CEO can be a direct shareholder — no requirement to use a holding company
Three structural options:
✓ RECOMMENDED
Option A
Individuals direct (Baru + Teru). Simplest, cleanest.
Option B
EXCEED REAL ESTATE LLC as parent. Undoes ring-fencing.
Option C
Other holding vehicle. Overkill at this stage.
3
Constitutional documents (MoA / AoA)
The Memorandum of Association + Articles of Association are the entity's "rulebook" — ownership %, decision-making, share-transfer rules, director powers. CVML drafts these as part of the AED 35,000 lumpsum.
✓ RECOMMENDED APPROACH
Wait for CVML's draft, then review. What you give them upfront is minimal:
  • Ownership split (e.g., Baru 50% / Teru 50%)
  • Named manager/director (probably one of you)
  • Confirm: single share class, no special voting rights
Let CVML draft a standard RAK ICC template based on those inputs. Review their draft, ask questions, ask for changes if anything looks unusual.
4
Flexibility for future subsidiaries / SPVs / investors
What this really means: don't paint yourself into a corner. The Holding Co should be able to absorb new SPVs, new shareholders, and restructuring later without having to rebuild from scratch.
📌 WHAT TO TELL CVML
Just give them this one instruction:
"Please structure EXCEED Capital with flexibility for future share issuance, additional shareholders, and adding subsidiary investments. Authorize a high share count so we don't have to amend the MoA every time we issue new shares."
That's it. CVML knows what this means and will draft accordingly. You don't need to specify anything else.

🧾 Regulatory & Tax obligations (ongoing)

Once the Holding Co is set up, these are the recurring obligations CVML flags:

📊 UAE Corporate Tax
Must register with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). 9% rate applies above the AED 375k profit threshold; QFZP exemption may apply for free-zone entities.
🧮 VAT registration
May be required if turnover crosses the mandatory threshold (AED 375k). Pure holding cos often don't, but CVML will assess.
🏛️ Economic Substance + UBO
Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) filings may apply — depends on activities + jurisdiction.
📅 Ongoing compliance
Annual corporate filings, license renewals, tax returns — all must be maintained or the entity goes inactive / penalised.
