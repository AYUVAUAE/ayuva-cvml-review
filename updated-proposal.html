🎯 Structure and Purpose (per CVML)

CVML describes the Holding Company as "the parent and central holding vehicle of the proposed structure" providing "strategic oversight, centralized control, and an efficient structure for future investments, expansion, and investor participation."

The Holding Company will:

  • Hold ownership interests in the Investment Company, Project Management Company, Asset / Hotel Management Company, and relevant SPVs
  • Act as the central ownership and governance vehicle of the group
  • Oversee strategic decision-making and group-level management
  • Facilitate future expansion, restructuring, and investor participation
  • Provide liability segregation and centralized asset ownership within the wider structure

Why it's a separate entity

Per the proposal: the Holding Co provides "an efficient and scalable framework for future investments, operations, and expansion." It is being established as a new UAE entity — not the existing EXCEED REAL ESTATE LLC — so that the AYUVA group sits in its own clean corporate envelope.

📋 Key Requirements (per CVML)

  • The minimum share capital will depend on the selected jurisdiction
  • The company will be required to appoint a manager / director and determine the appropriate shareholder structure
  • The constitutional documents will need to reflect the intended ownership, governance, and control framework
  • The structure must allow for flexibility in relation to future subsidiaries, SPVs, and investor participation

🧾 Regulatory & Tax Considerations (per CVML)

  • The Holding Company will be required to register for UAE Corporate Tax in accordance with applicable legislation
  • VAT registration may be required depending on the nature of activities and whether the applicable thresholds are met
  • Economic substance and beneficial ownership requirements may apply depending on the jurisdiction and activities undertaken
  • Ongoing compliance obligations will apply in relation to corporate filings, licensing renewals, and tax requirements

📑 Scope of Work CVML will deliver (Category A)

(i) Incorporation of the Company

  • Advising on the most suitable UAE jurisdiction (mainland or free zone) for the Holding Company
  • Assisting in the preparation and submission of the incorporation application
  • Drafting and/or reviewing all corporate and incorporation documents, including the Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association (where applicable), and ancillary documents
  • Advising on the most suitable ownership and governance structure
  • Liaising and coordinating with the relevant authorities on approvals, licensing, and registration
  • Following up until issuance of the trade license and incorporation documents
  • Providing general legal and practical guidance in relation to the incorporation

(ii) Corporate Structuring and Governance

  • Advising on the proposed group structure and ownership framework between the Holding Company and the underlying entities
  • Advising on the relationship between the Holding Company, Investment Company, PMC, AMC, and SPVs
  • Advising on governance mechanisms, shareholder arrangements, management structure, and decision-making framework
  • Drafting and/or reviewing any required shareholder resolutions and corporate approvals
  • Advising on future restructuring, expansion, and investor participation considerations
  • Preparing any ancillary legal documentation required for implementation of the structure

(iii) Corporate Tax and VAT Registration

  • Advising on the applicability of UAE Corporate Tax to the Holding Company's activities
  • Preparing and completing the Corporate Tax registration application with the FTA
  • Liaising with the FTA on any queries or clarifications
  • Assisting with VAT registration, where applicable
  • Providing high-level guidance on compliance requirements

🤔 What I need to decide on Section 1

Open questions for the Holding Company layer

  1. Pick a jurisdiction — DIFC, RAK ICC, RAK ICC SPV, or DWTC. CVML's notes call RAK ICC "suitable for a pure holding structure." DIFC is positioned for "high-end governance framework / institutional investment structures."
  2. Confirm the entity name — "EXCEED Capital" (or EXCEED Holdings, EXCEED Group, etc.)
  3. Manager / director — who will be appointed as the manager/director of the Holding Co?
  4. Shareholder structure — who holds shares in the Holding Co? (founders only, or with future investor allocation reserved?)
  5. Office — if DIFC or DWTC is chosen, decide on flexi-desk vs. dedicated office, since that's a separate cost.
  6. Visa allocation at this layer — does the Holding Co need any visas (e.g. for CEO)? If yes, DWTC has visa eligibility built in; otherwise the operating entities below will provide visas.
