🧾 Regulatory & Tax obligations (ongoing)

Once the Holding Co is set up, there are 4 recurring obligations that apply every year. These are NOT one-time — they recur for as long as the entity exists.

📊
1. UAE Corporate Tax
What it is: UAE introduced a 9% Corporate Tax in June 2023. Every UAE entity must register, regardless of profit level.
How it works:
• Profits below AED 375,000/year → 0% tax
• Profits above AED 375,000/year → 9% tax on the excess
For the Holding Co specifically: doesn't earn revenue itself (just owns shares). Likely zero or minimal taxable profit → likely 0% tax. CVML registers the entity with the FTA as part of the AED 35,000.
🧮
2. VAT registration
What it is: 5% Value Added Tax on goods/services sold in the UAE.
How it works:
• Mandatory if turnover crosses AED 375,000/year
• Voluntary if turnover crosses AED 187,500/year
For the Holding Co specifically: a pure holding company doesn't usually need VAT registration (no commercial sales). The Investment Co, PMC, AMC will likely need it; Holding Co probably won't. CVML will assess.
🏛️
3. Economic Substance (ESR) + UBO
Two separate filings, both annual.
Economic Substance Regulations (ESR):
• UAE law requires companies in "relevant activities" (banking, insurance, IP holding, headquarters, distribution, etc.) to prove they have real activity in the UAE — not just a paper company.
• Passive holding cos are typically out of scope (exempt).
• Still needs to file an annual notification saying "we don't do relevant activity."

Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO):
• UAE requires every entity to disclose who ultimately owns and controls the company.
• For EXCEED Capital: Baru + Teru filed as UBOs (each ≥25% ownership).
• One-time filing at setup, updated if ownership changes.
📅
4. Ongoing compliance
Routine annual maintenance to keep the entity alive:
• Trade license renewal — annual (~AED 1,500–2,500 for RAK ICC)
• Annual financial filings — books of account, possibly auditor's report
• Corporate Tax return — filed annually with FTA
• Annual general meeting / resolutions — board minutes, etc.
⚠ Miss these → entity goes into delinquent status → fines → eventually deregistered.

🏢 Office Requirement — Physical Mandatory

Unlike the Holding Co (RAK ICC, no office) and Investment Co (DIFC flexi-desk), PMC requires a real Ejari-registered office in mainland Dubai. No flexi-desk option for mainland DET.

⚠ Important: The DIFC office (used by Investment Co) CANNOT be the PMC's registered address. DIFC and Mainland are separate jurisdictions with separate office requirements. PMC needs its own mainland address.

✅ My recommendation

Set up PMC AFTER Investment Co — not immediately
